Weimin Huang

Welcome to my website! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Bistra Dilkina. I am affiliated with the USC Center for Artificial Intelligence in Society (CAIS) and the Artificial Intelligence Institute for Advances in Optimization (AI4OPT). During my studies, I spent time at Microsoft Research and the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory as a Ph.D. intern. Before joining USC, I received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the University of Toronto.

My research interests lie in machine learning, mathematical optimization, and data-driven decision-making. I develop scalable, efficient algorithms that exploit the structure of the problem and patterns in historical solution data, with applications to large-scale, real-world decision-making.
